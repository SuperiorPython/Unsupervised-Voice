# Group Projects — 60-second proof of concept

Source: script v7, the personal-story section, trimmed by deleting sentences. The only wording change is the first line, which joins two v7 lines so the clip stands alone. Everything else is v7 verbatim.

Length: 138 spoken words, about 55 to 64 seconds before pauses. If the take runs long, cut the "credit to the professor" line first.

Cues: `{mood, pose}` names come from `pipeline/video/src/avatar/Astronaut.tsx`. They are direction only and are never read aloud. Frame numbers get filled in after the voice take is recorded.

## What this tests (from rules.md, section 7)

1. Can the mood be read with the sound off? Rows 5 and 7 are the hardest tests: a flat face versus a big reaction.
2. Does it sound like Bryson? Record it unscripted first, then read it, and keep whichever phrasing is yours.
3. How many hours did it take, and how many were reusable assets?
4. Would Bryson watch it cold?
5. Show it to 3 to 5 people who haven't seen the channel, with no explanation. Ask what it was about and which line they'd repeat.

## Assets this clip needs

- All moods and poses above already exist. Nothing new is required.
- Optional: a folder prop for row 8.
- Voice: Bryson's real recording, in short chunks, a few takes per line. Pauses are set on the Remotion timeline, not by the recording alone.
